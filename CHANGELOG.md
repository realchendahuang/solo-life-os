## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-25

### Added
- Official public open-source release of Solo Life OS.
- Plain-text, local-first, Git-driven Life OS architecture for solopreneurs.
- Complete suite of native Agent Skills (.agents/skills/).
- 13 Red Lines writing constitution and 3 proven voice models (Street Hacker, Architect Deep Dive, Sharp Probe).
- Automated test suite with 199 passing contract and unit tests.
